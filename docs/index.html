Antarctic Meltwater Observatory

Surface meltwater on 12 Antarctic ice shelves, measured every melt season from free Sentinel-2 imagery, blind-validated, and cross-checked against Sentinel-1 radar. Meltwater ponding drives ice-shelf hydrofracture — the mechanism that destroyed Larsen B in 2002.

Radar × optical fusion — George VI melt states

Every 120 m cell classified per radar pass as DRY / WET / PONDED (ponded requires both sensors to agree; disagreements are counted as conflicts, never classified). The wet-to-ponded gap is the key number: only a small fraction of melt ever ponds — and ponding is what drives hydrofracture.
